Calendar

Events are visible to both parents. Any private note you add to an event is visible only to you — it is never sent to the other parent's device. The letter along the bottom of each date is who has her that day.

Printed record

A separate printed record from the Record tab — the custody schedule day by day, every swap request with its outcome, and calendar events, for a date range you choose. Private notes never print, for either parent.

Money

Each expense is split between you — 50/50 by default, adjustable per claim. Nothing is deleted or overwritten: a withdrawn claim stays visible, struck through, and a settlement is added to the record, never edited into an old total.

This is a balance, not a scoreboard. The number below is what is owed, worked out from the splits on each claim. It is never a comparison of how much either parent has spent — the two homes log at different rates for reasons that have nothing to do with the money.

The record

Nothing is deleted or overwritten: an edit is kept as a revision, and a withdrawn entry stays visible, struck through. Each entry carries the name chosen on the device that wrote it — the app cannot verify who typed it.
